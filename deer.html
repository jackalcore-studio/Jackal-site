DEERCORE

Deer deserve your attention.

Chronic wasting disease is spreading through deer, elk, moose, and other cervids, while most people still have no idea what it is. DEERCORE exists to make the science understandable, the problem harder to ignore, and public outreach easier to participate in.

The intent

Make the problem visible.

Make CWD better known

Translate difficult prion science into public-facing language without treating readers like they are stupid or pretending uncertainty does not exist.

Build public outreach

Create articles, short videos, printable zines, and practical resources that people can share in their own communities.

Support research

Point readers toward researchers, diagnostic programs, conservation work, and other credible efforts already tackling prion disease.

Support animal welfare

Treat affected animals as living beings worth protecting, not just statistics, hunting resources, or background wildlife.
